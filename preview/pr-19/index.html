Organoid Disease Modeling & Therapeutic Discovery类器官疾病建模与治疗策略研究

Duan Lab is a new organoid research laboratory. We aim to develop disease models to investigate mechanisms and evaluate therapeutic strategies.

Duan Lab 是一个新成立的类器官研究实验室，拟通过疾病模型研究发病机制并评估治疗策略。

Our Research我们的研究

The lab is building its research framework around two core areas: the development of novel disease models and microphysiological systems integrated with AI-enabled drug discovery. Specific models, methods, and projects will continue to evolve as the research progresses.

实验室围绕新型疾病模型开发、微生理系统与智能药物研发两大方向逐步建立研究体系。具体模型、方法与课题将随着研究推进持续完善。

01

Development of Novel Disease Models新型疾病模型开发

The lab aims to develop complementary disease-modeling approaches using complex organoids and tissue–immune humanized animal models to investigate immune dysfunction in infectious and tumor diseases. A longer-term goal is to establish a stepwise, human tissue-specific immune research platform spanning in vitro and in vivo models.

围绕复杂类器官与组织—免疫双人源化动物的互补模型，探索感染性疾病与肿瘤疾病中的免疫失效问题，并逐步建立从体外到体内的阶梯式人类组织特异性免疫研究平台。

02

Microphysiological Systems and AI-Enabled Drug Discovery微生理系统与智能药物研发

The lab aims to integrate microphysiological systems with artificial intelligence to explore more standardized, automated, and scalable approaches to drug research. Key areas include early biomarker identification and the integration of wet-lab experiments with computational analysis in a closed-loop workflow.

探索微生理系统与人工智能深度融合的研究体系，围绕方法学标准化、自动化与规模化、早期生物标志物识别，以及湿实验与计算分析闭环等方向开展研究。
